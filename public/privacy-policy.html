Kanjichō — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026 · Kanjichō version 1.0.0

Summary

Kanjichō is an offline-first kanji study app. It does not collect, transmit, share, or sell any personal data. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. All of your study progress is stored only on your own device and never leaves it. After the first load, the app makes no requests to any third-party server, and it can be used entirely offline.

What we do not collect

The app never collects or processes:

  • names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any contact details;
  • account credentials — the app has no accounts and no sign-in;
  • device identifiers, advertising IDs, or fingerprinting data;
  • precise or coarse location;
  • usage analytics, crash reports, or performance telemetry;
  • any content you type or study — quiz answers and review history are saved only in your device's local storage.

Data stored on your device

The following data is stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB database and localStorage (standard browser storage on your device, not accessible to us or to any third party). It exists only to make your own study progress work:

WhatWherePurpose
Review history and scheduling state for each kanji IndexedDB (local) Spaced-repetition scheduling of your quizzes and due reviews
Per-unit progress (seen / learned counts, streaks) IndexedDB (local) Progress indicators on the home screen
Display preferences (light/dark theme, kana vs. romaji answers) localStorage (local) Remember your settings between sessions

You can export all of this at any time with the in-app backup feature, delete it with the in-app “Reset progress” option, or remove it permanently by uninstalling the app / clearing site data. Because it never leaves your device, there is nothing for us to delete, return, or hand over on your behalf.

Network requests and third parties

Kanjichō serves all of its content — including all fonts — from its own origin. The app loads no third-party scripts, trackers, or advertising SDKs, and sends no information to any server. When you first open the app, your browser downloads the app's assets from our own web server; after that, everything is served from the on-device cache and the app works with the network completely disabled.

Purchases

Kanjichō version 1.0.0 contains no purchases, subscriptions, or paid features. If in-app purchases are added in a future version, they will be processed exclusively by Google Play Billing: Google (as the seller of record) receives and processes your payment information, and the developer receives no card numbers or bank details — only an anonymous purchase token used to deliver the purchased feature. This policy will be updated before any such feature is released.

Children and age

Kanjichō is an educational tool suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children, and is therefore designed to be compliant with children's-privacy rules such as COPPA by not collecting any data at all.

App permissions

The app requests no sensitive device permissions. It does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, storage files, or location.

Your rights and legal bases

Because no personal data is collected or processed, there is no personal data to which access, correction, deletion, portability, or objection rights would apply under regulations such as the GDPR or CCPA. If you have any privacy question or concern about the app, contact us using the email below and we will respond.

Security

Since study data never leaves your device and is not transmitted anywhere, the main security boundary is your device's own browser profile. We recommend using your device's screen lock and backing up your progress with the in-app export feature if you want a copy.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes anything described here, this page will be updated and the effective date above revised before that version is released.
